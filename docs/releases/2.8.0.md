## Levant 2.8.0

The app icon in Finder now follows your skin, just like the Dock icon.

- **The icon follows the skin everywhere.** Levant's icon in Applications, Launchpad and Spotlight now changes to match the skin in use, not just the Dock icon. With **Follow my Mac**, it changes when your Mac switches between light and dark. Oyster is Levant's own icon.
- **One switch for both.** **Settings → Appearance → App icon follows the skin** (on by default) controls the Dock icon and the Finder icon together. Turn it off to keep the Oyster icon everywhere.
- After an update, the icon is put back the next time Levant starts.

Your settings and data stay as they are.
