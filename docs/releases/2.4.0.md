**Quick Drop Zone 2.4.0**

- **A more distinctive look, from your new concept.** The sidebar now has a warm glow low on the left and a brass-edged highlight that slides between pages, a count badge (files waiting for you on Activity, loose files on Clean Up), and a colourful Apple Intelligence tile at the bottom. A slim bar across the top hides the sidebar, jumps to Find, and shows what's being watched.
- **Activity reads like a proper feed.** Big file icons, the name with its extension in small monospaced type, a tag for the folder it went to (and "Automatic" when it went by itself), the time at the top right, and Undo, Rename and Finder as small icon buttons that brighten under the pointer. Undo Last Move and Clear List sit beside the title, and the day headings are small capitals.
- Clean Up's main button is now the brass one.

Nothing about how files are sorted has changed, and every button still does what it did. Your favorite folders, notes, rules, learning and activity are kept.

Download `Quick-Drop-Zone-2.4.0.zip`, unzip it and drag Quick Drop Zone into Applications (choose Replace).
