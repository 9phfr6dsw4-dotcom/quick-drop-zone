**Quick Drop Zone 2.2.0**

- **A new look: graphite and brass.** The whole app is now dark, with brushed-graphite surfaces, thin brass details and softer cards. The main window has a sidebar (Activity, Clean Up, Find, Folders & Rules, Settings) with an Apple Intelligence status card at the bottom, and the menu-bar panel matches it.
- **Activity is grouped by day.** Today, Yesterday and earlier dates each get their own heading, and every entry shows the folder it went to as a small tag, with Undo, Rename and Show in Finder as neat pill buttons.
- **A new app icon that fills the whole icon.** It's now built the way macOS 26 expects, so it no longer sits on a grey plate in the Dock: a brushed-metal slab, a brass-rimmed tray and a frosted-glass tile with a glowing light.

Nothing about how files are sorted has changed. Your favorite folders, notes, rules, learning and activity are kept.

Download `Quick-Drop-Zone-2.2.0.zip`, unzip it and drag Quick Drop Zone into Applications (choose Replace).
