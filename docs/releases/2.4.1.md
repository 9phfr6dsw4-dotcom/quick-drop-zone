**Quick Drop Zone 2.4.1**

- **The menu-bar panel is tidier.** Buttons stay on one line, so the long "Rename to…" button no longer swells into a big oval. The new name is shown on its own line ("New name: …") and the button just says Rename. Each entry shows the full file name, why it was filed, tags for where it went, and small Undo, Rename and Finder icons on the right (hover for what each does).
- **One clear button at the bottom: "Open Quick Drop Zone".** Clean Up and Find moved into the ⋯ menu at the top, and Undo Last Move now sits beside "Recent".
- Buttons that wait for your decision (Move to…, Other Folder, Rename, Dismiss) wrap neatly onto a second line when there isn't room.

Nothing about how files are sorted has changed. Your favorite folders, notes, rules, learning and activity are kept.

Download `Quick-Drop-Zone-2.4.1.zip`, unzip it and drag Quick Drop Zone into Applications (choose Replace).
