## Levant 2.7.0

A new look. Nothing about how Levant files, renames or finds your files has changed.

- **New icon.** A polished steel bezel around a watch-style dial with a fine minute track, with the document sliding into the tray slot under a chevron.
- **Eight skins.** Slate and Oyster are the defaults, with Sky, Submariner, Turquoise, Bordeaux, Wisteria and Mint to choose from. Pick them in **Settings → Appearance**.
- **Follows your Mac, or doesn't.** By default Slate is used in dark mode and Oyster in light mode. Pick a skin while following your Mac to change which one is used for that mode, or switch to **Choose one skin** to use one skin whatever your Mac is set to.
- **The Dock icon follows the skin** (you can turn that off in Settings → Appearance).
- **Refined details:** serif page titles, a minute-track line under each title, and the new Apple Intelligence mark on the sidebar card.

Your settings and data stay as they are.
