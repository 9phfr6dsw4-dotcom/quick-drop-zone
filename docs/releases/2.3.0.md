**Quick Drop Zone 2.3.0**

- **The app icon is fixed.** 2.2.0's icon came out black because its layers were stacked the wrong way round. It's now rebuilt from your new concept: a brushed-titanium bezel, a recessed chamber ringed with dial ticks, a frosted-glass document with a curled corner sliding into a tray, and a champagne chevron.
- **A more refined look.** Cards are lit from above with a fine edge and soft shadow. The sidebar has the app icon and name at the top, a brass marker on the page you're on, and highlights as you move over it. Activity shows each file's own icon (with a small brass spark on files filed automatically), rows that brighten under the pointer, and a small ruler under each page title. The menu-bar panel's drop area is now a recessed tray that glows when you hold files over it. Settings uses proper switches.

Nothing about how files are sorted has changed. Your favorite folders, notes, rules, learning and activity are kept.

Download `Quick-Drop-Zone-2.3.0.zip`, unzip it and drag Quick Drop Zone into Applications (choose Replace).
